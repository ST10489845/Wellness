University student support

Find out whether your institution offers student counselling, wellness services or referrals.

Academic support

YOUR PERSONAL SPACE

Privacy & boundaries

Understand what this prototype stores and how to use it thoughtfully.

What is stored

Your check-in answers, optional note and date are saved in this browser using local storage.

What is not stored

This prototype does not send your entries to a university, lecturer or online server.

Using a shared device

Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view saved entries. Avoid entering sensitive personal information on a shared device.

What this website is not

This is a personal reflection tool. It is not a diagnosis, treatment plan or emergency service.

Getting support

If you need help, contact your institution's student support service or a qualified professional. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
